What Kairos does

Kairos sits between your music apps and your speakers. It listens to the apps you choose, quietens them, splits the sound into vocals, drums, bass and everything else, and plays your mix a few seconds later. All of it happens on your Mac.

Choose what to hear

Pick any app, several, or all audio. Switch instantly between Original, Vocals only and Instrumental. Standby plays the untouched song, from the menu bar or with ⌥⇧B.

Shape every part

Set the level of each part, or mute or solo it. Clean-up quietens the noise around a voice. Bass, mids and treble. Pitch up or down 12 semitones without changing tempo. Stereo width from mono to extra wide. A built-in limiter keeps it from clipping.

Make it yours

Now Playing shows the artwork and takes its colours from the album. Light, dark or artwork themes. Saved setups, including Karaoke, Gym and Vocals focus. Lives in the menu bar.

Good to know

Private
Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. The separation model runs on your Mac.
A few seconds behind
Kairos plays a few seconds behind the source. Great for music; video will be out of sync.
Permissions
macOS asks once to let Kairos record audio, and once per music app for song info.
Install
Open the DMG and drag Kairos to Applications.
First time opening Kairos
If macOS says it can’t verify Kairos, click Done, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.
